## Observed core visual identity
- Teaching segments alternate facecam, full-frame navy/very dark purple infographics and factual examples.
- Slides: dark navy background, off-white/gray type and diagram lines; **strong selective yellow** headings/key words and large anchored numbers. Green can support a source-grounded increase on a chart.
- White/yellow very bold spoken keywords over facecam; some videos use comparatively unadorned facecam for several seconds.
- Dynamic **builds** of teaching scenes: establish the canvas, reveal a label/item, reveal arrows/relations, show next consequence, and RETURN to the speaker.
- Full-screen chart or diagram is allowed to stay up across several spoken clauses without cuts. An infograph is a scene, NOT a tiny sticker at the right of the face.
- Examples documented: 5-step list, growth axes/curve, MEDIA MODEL vs BUSINESS MODEL sponsor tree, retention breakdown/calendar, a branching strategy graphic, video-thumbnail proof montages.

## Composition building blocks
- HEAD — original talking-head with selective bold facecam captions (not every word gets an oversized title).
- INTRO_PREMISE — a short contrast/hook with one emphasized verified key phrase.
- NAVY_TITLE — full-screen navy concept card.
- STEP_LIST — 2+ real transcript-grounded enumerated steps, reveal one at a time.
- SIDE_BY_SIDE — two factual conceptual groups, separator, icons/arrows revealing precisely when verbally introduced.
- DATA_GRAPH — values and curve only from evidenced dataset; don't visually imply fabricated performance.
- AUTHENTIC_PROOF — relevant authorized company/product/document/screenshot; no reference creator video reused.
- CONCEPT_TREE — a logical relationship full screen, sequential links; hold on screen long enough to read.
- FACE_RETURN — return to speaker with natural cut, no gratuitous zoom.

## Production algorithm
1. Understand each complete segment, pick one or no block; don't hit a frequency quota.
2. Obtain word-level timestamps and check cut removals against audio.
3. When the speaker **explains a mechanism**, construct a diagram based on exactly those claims.
4. Author post-cut output-timeline events: startMs,endMs,scene_type,evidence_words,diagram_nodes,links,asset_rights,protectedRegions.
5. Validate geometry in the original ratio. If 16:9 original, 16:9 final and full-frame graphics.
6. Export with HyperFrames; combine with original audio and video without voice substitution.
7. QA phone readability, authentic evidence, native format, clip transitions and absence of UI screenshot chrome.

## Failure conditions
- Four creator models with identical motion except palette.
- Small sidebar/boxed slide over talking head when diagram must be full screen.
- Unverified claims, fabricated figures, screenshots, rankings or historical data.
- Fake chat bubbles, white-letterbox landscape, wrong original voice.
- Infographics where a person's actual sentence is not reflected in the diagram.
- Montage only 12s where user asked for full 44s or longer.
