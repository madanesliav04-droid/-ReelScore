# Edit+ Creative North Star — 4 creator-inspired engines

**Vision.** An uploaded raw video must come back as if an experienced editor had understood its narrative, gestures and purpose. Every cut, shot change, title, infographic, sound cue and licensed B-roll must have a clearly logged editorial reason. Liav is the creator / brand; OMNI is the environment and is not the person's identity. Format-native input/output (horizontal stays horizontal unless user overrides); original voice and phrasing untouched.

### Motion renderer
HyperFrames handles deterministic video composition, not automated script comprehension. An Edit+ orchestrator must provide: a verified transcript, true timing after cuts, protected face/hand regions, evidence for onscreen words and footage licenses. HyperFrames renders the chosen visual plan. Do not claim a demo animation has become an autonomous editor.

### Acceptance
Compare 4 videos built from the same raw 910×512 landscape talking-head clip (sample evidence “aider / valeur / résoudre”). They must differ in **decision logic, scene grammar, cut rhythm, b-roll policy and hierarchy**. Keep all unsourced numerals out. Preserve the original voice. Test a second portrait and a gesture-intensive video before saying “production ready”.
